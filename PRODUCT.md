## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Blazor WebAssembly application on .NET 8.

## Users

Primary users are Agile and delivery coaches, engineering and delivery leaders, Scrum Masters and team facilitators, transformation leaders, and organisational improvement practitioners.

They use the platform when they need to understand how their organisation actually delivers work and identify practical ways to improve it. The platform supports organisations using Agile, Lean, product-oriented, or hybrid operating models.

## Product Purpose

Operational Intelligence Hub helps leaders and practitioners observe, interpret, and improve organisational delivery systems.

It brings together signals from across the delivery system, interprets them as a whole, and connects observations to practical improvement actions. It moves users from guessing, through understanding, to improving. Success means users can identify systemic patterns and take meaningful improvement action rather than relying on isolated metrics or additional reporting.

## Positioning

The platform provides operational intelligence rather than activity dashboards, isolated metrics, or compliance reporting. Its Insight Engine interprets combined operational signals similarly to how an experienced coach analyses a delivery system, surfacing relationships, potential influences, and improvement opportunities.

## Operating Context

The ecosystem has four connected layers:

1. Perspectives: knowledge, frameworks, and thought leadership.
2. Operational Intelligence Hub: diagnostics and interpreted insights.
3. Improvement Plays: practical interventions, experiments, and coaching techniques.
4. Advisory Services: coaching and consulting for deeper organisational change.

Within the Hub, users work across Diagnostics, Intelligence (the Coach Hub), and Organisation tools. Diagnostics capture signals; the Coach Hub interprets them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Diagnostics capture structured signals from delivery systems, including backlog health, team health, and organisational maturity.
- The Insight Engine combines signals across operational intelligence domains and produces observations and interpretations rather than raw metric reporting.
- The improvement layer connects observations to facilitation formats, improvement plays, coaching techniques, and team experiments.
- Internal intelligence domains are Flow, Team, Capability, Organisational, and Cultural Intelligence. Users should primarily experience insights and observations rather than domain categories.
- The open platform supports manually entered data and periodic diagnostics.
- Future professional capabilities may include expanded history, deeper insights, cross-team comparisons, and trend analysis.
- Future enterprise capability may analyse live operational data across teams and portfolios.
- The platform is methodology-neutral across Agile, Lean, product-oriented, and hybrid models.
- Product tiering and live-system integrations remain future evolution areas; do not present them as currently available without evidence.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Operational Intelligence Hub. The experience should be coaching-oriented: observations are guidance rather than judgement.

## Product Principles

- System thinking: understand delivery performance as the result of interactions among teams, practices, structures, and culture.
- Insight over reporting: prioritise interpretation and meaning over isolated metrics and compliance dashboards.
- Coaching-oriented design: present observations as guidance, not judgement.
- Practical improvement: connect every meaningful insight to techniques, plays, or experiments that can be tried.
- Methodology neutrality: support varied operating models without assuming one delivery method.
