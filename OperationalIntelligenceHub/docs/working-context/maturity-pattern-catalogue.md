# Maturity Assessment — Pattern Catalogue (Working Draft)

## Status

This is the working catalogue from the Rule Register Review. These are candidate patterns for interpreting selected maturity behaviours; they are not approved or executable detection rules. See [pattern definitions](./maturity-pattern-definitions.md) and [pattern detection rules](./maturity-pattern-detection-rules.md).

The register names 26 patterns. Its closing note refers to 27 candidates, but no 27th ID is present. The catalogue therefore preserves the 26 named IDs and leaves the count discrepancy open rather than adding an inferred pattern.

## Scope

- **EM** patterns explore differences in roles' assumptions and expectations.
- **BF** patterns explore possible friction in relationships, decision rights, value translation, learning, and collaboration.
- **CC** patterns explore conditions in which leadership or organisational arrangements may constrain capability.
- **SC** patterns explore tensions between stated intent and other selected behaviours.
- **PR** patterns describe potentially reinforcing conditions; they do not prove that delivery is effective or free of problems.

The patterns are hypotheses to validate with users. A difference in role, theme, or maturity level is not by itself evidence that a pattern exists.
